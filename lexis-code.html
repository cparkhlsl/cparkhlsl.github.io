Anatomy of Code on Lexis

How to use:

Click the red rectangles to view information about each annotated feature.

Notes to Decisions
The Notes to Decisions is a curated list of cases that interpret the statute's language, organized by important sub-issues related to the statute.
History
The History tab includes information on earlier versions of the statute, including summaries of changes made by amendments to the statute.
Citing Decisions
The Citing Decisions lists all the cases that cite the statute available in Lexis.
Other Citing Sources
Other Citing Sources lists all the non-case sources that cite the statute in Lexis.
Table of Contents
The Table of Contents allows researchers to see the surrounding statutes that may be relevant to their research problem.
Caption or Heading
The Caption or Heading tells researchers where they're located in the code (Title, Subtitle, Chapter, and Individual Section) so they can assess that they're in the appropriate place for their research issue.
Currentness
Currentness tells researchers when Lexis last updated any changes to the statute made by the legislature; typically, Lexis updates regularly and is current within a week.
Statutory History Line
The Statutory History Line lets researchers know when the statute was originally enacted and whether it has ever been amended.
