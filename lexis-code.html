Notes of Decisions
The Notes of Decisions is a curated list of cases that interpret the statute's language, organized by important sub-issues related to the statute.
History
The History tab includes information on earlier version of the statute, including summaries of changes made by amendments to the statute.
Citing References
The Citing References tab lists all sources available in Westlaw, including cases, secondary sources, and more, that cite the statute.
Context & Analysis
The Context & Analysis tab is a curated list of non-case sources that may be helpful for gaining helpful context on the statute, including cross-references to related statutes and secondary sources.
Table of Contents
The Table of Contents allows researchers to see the surrounding statutes that may be relevant to their research problem.
Caption or Heading
The Caption or Heading tells researchers where they're located in the code (Title, Subtitle, Chapter, and Individual Section) so they can assess that they're in the appropriate place for their research issue.
Currentness
Currentness tells researchers when Westlaw last updated any changes to the statute made by the legislature; typically, Westlaw updates regularly, and is typically current within a week.
Statutory History Line
The Statutory History Line lets researchers know when the statute was originally enacted and whether it's ever been amended.
